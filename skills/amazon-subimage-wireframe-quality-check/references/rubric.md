# Wireframe QC Rubric

Score each item 0-2.

## Role Clarity

- 0: Image role is unclear or duplicates another image.
- 1: Role is present but not tied to purchase decision.
- 2: Role is specific and necessary in the sequence.

## Composition Diversity

- 0: Same layout as most other images.
- 1: Some variation but same information-card skeleton.
- 2: Distinct composition pattern and visual structure.

## Visual Hierarchy

- 0: All elements have similar weight.
- 1: Main visual or main copy is identifiable but weak.
- 2: Primary, secondary, and support elements are obvious.

## Photo/Material Specificity

- 0: Generic placeholder only.
- 1: Subject is named but angle/context is vague.
- 2: Subject, angle/context, and production source are clear.

## Copy Quality

- 0: Too much copy, risky claim, or unclear promise.
- 1: Mostly usable but needs trimming or risk mitigation.
- 2: Short, claim-safe, and aligned with the visual.

## Figma Editability

- 0: Raster-only or not separated.
- 1: Partly editable but hard to revise.
- 2: Text, shapes, badges, placeholders, and notes are separate layers.

## Designer Handoff

- 0: Designer must invent the design.
- 1: Designer can infer direction but must ask questions.
- 2: Designer can begin layout, asset collection, and design polish immediately.
