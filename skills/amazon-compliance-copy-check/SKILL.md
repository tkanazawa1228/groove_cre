# Amazon Compliance Copy Check

Use this skill as a creative risk screen, not as legal advice. For current legal or platform-rule determinations, verify current official sources and state that final approval belongs to the client/legal/review team.

## Review Priorities

Check for:

- Medical, therapeutic, disease prevention, or immune-effect implications
- Beauty/health efficacy claims without permitted substantiation
- Infant, pregnancy, nursing, or child safety implications
- Breast milk superiority or inferiority implications for infant formula
- No.1, best, highest, safest, guaranteed, complete, perfect, absolute claims
- Competitor disparagement
- Review-derived claims that exceed the original evidence
- Before/after exaggeration
- Certification, official approval, or expert recommendation without proof
- AI-generated logos, marks, labels, or package claims

Read `references/category-risk.md` when category-specific risk matters.

## Workflow

1. List every claim-bearing phrase.
2. Classify each as `ok`, `caution`, `revise`, or `avoid`.
3. Explain the risk in plain language.
4. Provide safer alternatives that preserve the selling point.
5. Mark claims requiring source confirmation.
6. Do not invent substantiation.

## Safer Rewrite Principles

- Convert effect claims into handling, usability, or experience claims when appropriate.
- Use softer handling and usability language such as `easy to use`, `supports routine`, `reduces prep effort`, `simple to store`, or `helps organize` instead of guarantees.
- Prefer factual specs with source: quantity, size, capacity, period, included items, material.
- Keep comparison neutral: compare situations or formats, not brand superiority.
- Remove unsupported superlatives unless the source and allowable wording are provided.

## Output Format

| Text | Risk | Reason | Safer Alternative | Source Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
