# Category Risk Notes

Use these as creative screening heuristics. Check current official rules for final legal/platform guidance.

## Infant Formula / Baby Food

High-risk areas:

- Breast milk superiority/inferiority comparisons
- Disease prevention, immunity, allergy, growth, development, sleep, digestion, or health guarantees
- Ingredient effects tied to disease or body function outcomes
- Fear-based claims about infant health
- Expert/official recommendation without permission

Safer directions:

- Preparation convenience
- Portioning and handling
- Storage, portability, and stock management
- Factual package/spec information
- Parent workflow and usage scene, without promising infant outcomes

## Food / Beverage

High-risk areas:

- Disease prevention or treatment
- Body function claims without permitted category support
- Detox, fatigue recovery, immunity, weight-loss guarantees
- Unverified nutrition superiority

Safer directions:

- Taste, format, convenience, storage, portion, use occasion
- Factual nutrition or ingredient information from label
- Routine or lifestyle fit without health guarantees

## Supplements / Functional Foods

High-risk areas:

- Drug-like efficacy
- Claims beyond approved notification wording
- Before/after body changes
- Doctor/expert recommendation without substantiation

Safer directions:

- Use only approved/label wording when available
- Put required disclaimers if instructed by the source
- Keep mechanism education conservative

## Cosmetics / Beauty

High-risk areas:

- Permanent change, medical treatment, cell-level repair, whitening beyond permitted scope
- Before/after exaggeration
- Guaranteed results

Safer directions:

- Texture, fragrance, use feel, makeup effect, routine fit
- Permitted cosmetic effect wording only

## General Amazon Creative

High-risk areas:

- Unsupported No.1 or highest claims
- Competitor disparagement
- Misleading scale or quantity visuals
- Fake badges, fake certifications, fake labels
- Review quotes without care

Safer directions:

- Source-backed facts
- User problem framing
- Neutral comparisons
- Concrete usage scenes
